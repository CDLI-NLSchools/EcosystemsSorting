Ecosystems Sorting

Grade 7 STEAM, Exploring Ecosystems, Lesson 2

For each ecosystem, choose the factors you think you would find there and arrange them in the scene. The same bank of pictures is used on every page, and many factors belong in more than one ecosystem. On the Interactions page, build one example of each kind of interaction, then describe the interaction you showed.

Drag a picture into the scene and drag it to move it. You can also place a picture by selecting it and pressing Enter, move a placed picture with the arrow keys, and send it back to the bank with the Delete key or the x button.
